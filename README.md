One sentence pitching the worst, most useless app imaginable, for the
make.fun bounty
`u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23`.

## The entry

> Judge, meet SnoozeMail: an app that reads your unread emails aloud in
> a soothing voice while you sleep, so you wake up fully briefed and
> completely unrested, for five dollars a month, silence sold separately.

The app: SnoozeMail, which reads your unread emails aloud while you
sleep. The founder-certain pitch: you wake up fully briefed. The
perfectly pointless payoff: you are also completely unrested — and
silence costs extra. The line speaks to the judge directly, making the
judge the first person pitched.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the one sentence above to `/output/entry.txt`
(1–240 characters, UTF-8, exactly one sentence). No network, no
randomness, no dependencies.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
